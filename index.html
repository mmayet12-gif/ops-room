Chapter 2

It talks to me like a person with a short attention span

One rule: the Secretary is the only part that is allowed to contact me, and it batches questions into a morning brief and an evening check-in. Tap a command to see what comes back (example replies).

life-osntfy
  • No app of its own: it uses a free push-notification service and one private channel.
  • Alerts never carry rand amounts or personal figures.
  • Anything urgent goes out at once; everything else waits for the next brief.
  • If a tool fails three times it pauses itself and asks me. A short internet drop no longer counts as a failure.

Chapter 4

Three things this taught me

96%

Cache first

One long chat with browsing and video stills used 96% of a week's usage. Now a finished match is fetched once, saved, and read from disk.

1.5 GB

Measure, don't guess

My first RAM alarm used a fixed limit that a browser plus a model crossed all day. Alarming on free memory (under 1.5 GB) stopped the noise.

1 inbox

Let it ask me

It has one inbox for me, not forty. Repeats collapse into one open question with a running total, so my phone stays useful.

Your turn

Build a small one

  1. Pick one chore you repeat every week. Mine was the Saturday match report.
  2. Give it one queue and one voice. Jobs go through a queue; only one thing may message you.
  3. Use free local models for the slow, overnight work and keep the paid model for the final judgement.
  4. Add a test for every bug. If it broke once, it should never break the same way twice.
