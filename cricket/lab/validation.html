Checks

Does it hold up?

Each claim and metric in Ganjoo's primer, tested on our own data. Each verdict follows a rule stated next to its test. "Not confirmed" means our test did not reproduce it, which can be our implementation as much as the claim.

How the testing works

  1. Check the data first: match counts, totals that reconcile, what is left out (rain-affected and no-result games are not used for fitting).
  2. Say what would prove it wrong: each card shows its verdict rule. Two par rules were tightened after a first run, which made our own club-cricket result look worse, not better.
  3. Hold data out: par, the curve, the RAA baseline and win probability are refitted on a random 70% of matches and scored on the other 30%.
  4. Report the misses, including ours.
