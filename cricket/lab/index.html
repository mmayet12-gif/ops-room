How it works, in four lines

  1. Par: each ground's expected first-innings total, shrunk towards its season and competition when the sample is small.
  2. Resource curve: expected runs still to come from any state, fitted Duckworth-Lewis style and stretched to the match's par.
  3. Value of a ball: RAA compares runs with the average from the same state; Impact adds the change in the curve, so wickets cost runs.
  4. Win probability: projected total over the expected total, raised to a power and checked against outcomes; WPA is each ball's change in it.

Full formulas, our choices where the primer is silent, and known limits are on the Methods page.
