2 · What is one ball worth?

Impact, the primer's runs added: the runs off the ball plus the change in expected runs still to come. A wicket costs the runs the curve says that wicket would have helped make.

3 · Win probability from the scoreboard

Score, balls left, wickets down and par only: no players, no form, no odds. It reads the scoreboard; it does not forecast a match.
