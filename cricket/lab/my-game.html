My game, in context

Last Man Stands is 8 a side, 20 overs of 5 balls, and the last batter can bat alone. The same par, curve, RAA, Impact and win-probability methods are refitted on Johannesburg/Sandton league games, then used to look at my bowling and batting and my team's plan. Small samples throughout: read these as descriptions, not verdicts.

Why are my spells short?

"Short" here means overs per game. (The data has no ball tracking, so it cannot say anything about the length I pitch the ball.) What the scorebook can show is how many overs I get, when, and how they went compared with the rest of our attack. It cannot show the captain's reasons.
