Resource curve and par scores

How many more runs does an average side score from here? The curve answers that for every balls-left and wickets-down state. Par says how easy the conditions are; a higher par stretches the curve.

Par by ground and season

Par is the raw first-innings average pulled towards the season average, by an amount that depends on how many innings the ground has had (n) and how noisy totals are (k). Small n: trust the parent; large n: trust the ground.
