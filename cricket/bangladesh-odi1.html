Four findings

Key findings

  • The middle overs are where the two batting sides differ most. Since 2024 South Africa scored 5.96 an over in overs 11-40 (4,457 balls) and Bangladesh 4.79 (4,425 balls); in the powerplay the two were close (5.3 and 5.44).
  • Bangladesh's bowlers have been the cheaper attack in every phase since 2024: economy 4.7, 4.7 and 6.44 in the powerplay, middle and death overs, against South Africa's 5.51, 5.68 and 8.35. The two sides played different opponents and grounds, so this is each attack's own record, not a like-for-like contest.
  • Two Bangladesh bowlers carry their phases: Taskin Ahmed took 19 powerplay wickets at 4.2 an over (474 balls) and Mehidy Hasan Miraz bowled 1,139 middle-over balls at 4.1 an over (20 wickets), both since 2024.
  • Buffalo Park has a thin, old ODI record: 9 ODIs in the database, the latest on 2023-03-18. The first innings averaged 242 and the second 193; South Africa won 6 of their 7 there. Head-to-head ODIs in the database: South Africa 5, Bangladesh 2 (in 2022, the latest series in South Africa, Bangladesh won 2 of 3).

Method and sources

Where every number comes from

Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet is loaded into a local SQLite database. This page is generated from the output of python -m src.analysis.odi1_dossier; the build stops if it differs from the reviewed document, if any table row is missing from the page, or if the page has a table the document does not have.

Generated by python -m src.analysis.odi1_dossier. Data: Cricsheet.org (ball-by-ball, every ODI in the database). Form window: ODIs since 2024-01-01. ODI phases by over: powerplay 1-10, middle 11-40, death 41-50. Descriptive only: patterns from past ODIs, no forecast of this one.

Limits

  • Bowler types are known for only part of the balls (the coverage line under each match-up table gives the share); untyped bowlers are never guessed.
  • Buffalo Park has nine ODIs in the database, the latest in 2023: a thin and old venue sample.
  • Cricsheet has no shot coordinates, pitch maps or ball speeds, so there are no zone or line-and-length charts.
  • Nothing adjusts for the strength of the opposition; the two sides played different opponents since 2024.
